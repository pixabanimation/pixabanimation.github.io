# Samsung Galaxy Book5 Pro Review: The Ultimate Copilot+ AI Laptop?

The landscape of mobile computing is shifting. We are no longer just looking at raw CPU clock speeds or RAM capacity; we are looking at NPU (Neural Processing Unit) performance. As artificial intelligence integrates into every layer of our workflow, the hardware requirements for creative professionals are evolving. Samsung has entered this new era with the Samsung Galaxy Book5 Pro, a machine designed specifically to bridge the gap between traditional productivity and the new age of Copilot+ AI integration.

For motion designers, video editors, and digital artists, a laptop is more than just a tool; it is an extension of the creative process. You need color accuracy, high-speed rendering, and enough "intelligence" to handle AI-driven tasks without breaking a sweat. In this review, we will dissect whether the Galaxy Book5 Pro lives up to the hype of being a premier "AI PC" or if it is simply another iterative update in Samsung's sleek lineup.

The New Era of Hardware: Copilot+ and the NPU

The headline feature of the Galaxy Book5 Pro is its designation as a Copilot+ PC. This isn't just marketing fluff; it signifies a fundamental change in how the laptop interacts with Windows 11. The heart of this machine is the latest generation of silicon designed with a heavy emphasis on the NPU.

Traditional laptops rely on the CPU for general tasks and the GPU for heavy rendering. However, AI tasks—like real-time background blur in video calls, live captions, or local image generation—are incredibly taxing on these components. The dedicated NPU in the Galaxy Book5 Pro handles these "small" AI tasks efficiently, allowing your CPU and GPU to stay focused on your heavy-duty animation or video editing software.

For creators using Adobe Creative Cloud or DaVinci Resolve, this means smoother performance. As these software suites increasingly integrate AI-driven features like "Generative Fill" or "Auto Reframe," having a dedicated NPU ensures that your hardware doesn't choke when you trigger an AI command.

Visual Excellence: The Dynamic AMOLED 2X Display

Advertisement

As motion designers, our eyes are our most important tools. A laptop with poor color reproduction can lead to hours of wasted time in the color grading stage. Samsung, being a leader in display technology, has not held back here. The Galaxy Book5 Pro features a Dynamic AMOLED 2X display that sets a new standard for thin-and-light laptops.

The color accuracy is breathtaking. With support for high DCI-P3 color gamuts, the transitions in gradients are buttery smooth, and the blacks are deep and true. This is critical when you are working on high-contrast motion graphics or cinematic color grading. The high refresh rate (up to 120Hz) ensures that motion feels fluid, which is vital when you are reviewing your animations frame-by-frame.

Furthermore, the brightness levels are sufficient for working in various lighting environments. While it might not replace a high-end desktop monitor in a studio setting, it is arguably one of the best portable displays for creative professionals on the move. If you are working from a cafe or a client site, you can trust what you see on this screen.

Performance for Motion Designers and Editors

While the AI features grab the headlines, the "meat" of the laptop lies in its ability to handle professional software. The Intel Core Ultra architecture inside the Galaxy Book5 Pro is designed for a hybrid workflow. It balances power efficiency with burst performance, which is essential when you are rendering a complex After Effects composition.

In our testing, the laptop handled multi-layered compositions with ease. While it may not replace a high-end workstation with a dedicated RTX 4090 GPU for heavy 3D rendering, it is remarkably capable for 2D and light 3D motion design. The integration of high-speed LPDDR5X RAM ensures that multitasking—switching from Premiere Pro to Chrome with 50 tabs open—is seamless.

One thing to note is the thermal management. Because the chassis is incredibly thin, the fans do kick in during heavy renders. However, Samsung has optimized the fan curves to be relatively quiet, ensuring that your focus remains on the creative process rather than the sound of whirring blades.

Workflow Optimization Tips

To get the most out of this machine, we recommend the following:

* Enable AI-Enhanced Features: Take advantage of Windows Copilot for quick text summaries or image generation to speed up your mood boarding.

* Use External Displays for Heavy 3D: While the AMOLED is great, for heavy 3D work, connect to a high-powered monitor to offload some visual processing.

* Optimize Power Settings: When rendering, switch to "Best Performance" mode to ensure the NPU and CPU are fully utilized.

The Ecosystem Advantage: Samsung Galaxy Integration

Advertisement

If you are already a part of the Samsung Galaxy ecosystem, the Galaxy Book5 Pro feels like a natural extension of your digital life. The "Multi Control" feature is a game-changer. It allows you to use your laptop's mouse and keyboard to control your Galaxy Tab or even your smartphone.

Imagine you are designing a character on your tablet using a stylus. With Multi Control, you can simply drag that image from your tablet directly into your After Effects timeline on the laptop. This level of seamless integration reduces the friction between devices, allowing for a more fluid creative flow.

Additionally, the "Second Screen" feature allows you to use a Galaxy Tab as a portable secondary monitor. For motion designers, having a second screen for your timeline or your reference images is a massive productivity boost. This ecosystem synergy is something Apple has mastered with the iPad/Mac integration, and Samsung is finally catching up with a robust, Windows-based solution.

Design, Portability, and Build Quality

The Galaxy Book5 Pro is an exercise in minimalism. It is incredibly thin and remarkably light for the amount of power it packs. The build quality feels premium, with a sturdy aluminum chassis that resists flex. It feels like a professional tool, not a consumer toy.

The keyboard is tactile and offers decent travel for a thin laptop, making long coding or scripting sessions (for expression-heavy animations) much more comfortable. The trackpad is expansive and responsive, which is helpful when you are navigating complex timelines with precision.

However, there is a trade-off to this thinness. The port selection is somewhat limited. You will likely need to carry a USB-C hub if you plan on connecting multiple peripherals like external SSDs, Wacom tablets, and extra monitors. This is a common theme in modern ultra-portables, but it is something to keep in mind if your studio setup relies heavily on legacy USB-A connections.

Conclusion: Is It Worth the Investment?

The Samsung Galaxy Book5 Pro is a sophisticated machine that aims to redefine what a "creative laptop" looks like in the age of AI. It isn't just about raw power; it's about intelligent power. The combination of a stunning AMOLED display, a dedicated NPU for Copilot+ tasks, and seamless ecosystem integration makes it a formidable contender for any professional on the move.

If you are a motion designer who needs color accuracy and portability, or a tech enthusiast eager to dive into the world of local AI processing, this laptop is a top-tier choice. While it may not replace a desktop workstation for the most intensive 3D tasks, it is perhaps the most capable "daily driver" for the modern, AI-integrated creative professional.
